# GDS demo geometry

All three files share one coordinate system: a 2000 x 2000 um (2 x 2 mm)
InP HBT test chip, origin at the chip's bottom-left corner, 1 user unit =
1 um, 1 database unit = 1 nm (`UNITS` record = `1e-3, 1e-9`). Generated by
`guide/_data/_gen/gen_gds_demo.py`; verified against the app's own parser in
`guide/_data/_gen/verify_gds_demo.py` (see `VERIFY.md`).

Chip extent (all three files): **(0, 0) - (2000, 2000) um**.

## Alignment marks (shared, all four corners)

Cross ("plus-sign") marks, arm length 40 um, arm width 8 um, centered at:

| mark | x (um) | y (um) |
|---|---|---|
| bottom-left (BL) | 100.0 | 100.0 |
| bottom-right (BR) | 1900.0 | 100.0 |
| top-left (TL) | 100.0 | 1900.0 |
| top-right (TR) | 1900.0 | 1900.0 |

Each mark's own bounding box is (cx-20, cy-4)-(cx+20, cy+4) union
(cx-4, cy-20)-(cx+4, cy+20), area 576.0 um^2 (2 x 320 - 64 overlap).

## `first_exposure.gds`

- Layers: layer 1 only. Datatype 0 = alignment marks, 1 = base pads,
  2 = emitter fingers, 3 = collector pads.
- Cell `TOP`, 22 polygons total, 31374.0 um^2 total pattern area.
- 4 alignment marks (above), 576.0 um^2 each, 2304.0 um^2 total.
- 6 devices in a 2-row x 3-column array, 250 um pitch in x, 300 um pitch in
  y, each: base pad 40 x 60 um, emitter finger W x 10 um (W = 4 or 5 um,
  alternating), collector pad 40 x 60 um, pad-to-emitter-center offset 50 um
  (pad inner edge 30 um from device center + 20 um half-pad-width — see
  `PAD_GAP`/`PAD_W` in the generator).

| device | cx (um) | cy (um) | emitter W (um) | footprint area (um^2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 800.0 | 850.0 | 4.0 | 4840.0 |
| D2 | 1050.0 | 850.0 | 5.0 | 4850.0 |
| D3 | 1300.0 | 850.0 | 4.0 | 4840.0 |
| D4 | 800.0 | 1150.0 | 5.0 | 4850.0 |
| D5 | 1050.0 | 1150.0 | 4.0 | 4840.0 |
| D6 | 1300.0 | 1150.0 | 5.0 | 4850.0 |

Device array bounding box: (730, 820) - (1370, 1180) um.

## `second_exposure.gds`

- Layer 1: the identical 4 alignment marks, identical coordinates, for
  mark-based alignment to `first_exposure.gds`.
- Layer 2: interconnect metal (datatype 1) + airbridge posts (datatype 2),
  registered to the same 6 device centers as `first_exposure.gds`.
  - Metal bridge per device: 140 x 10 um, centered at (cx, cy+40).
  - Two airbridge posts per device: 10 x 10 um, centered at
    (cx-70, cy+40) and (cx+70, cy+40) — landing on the bridge ends.
- Cell `TOP`, 22 polygons total (4 marks + 6 bridges + 12 posts),
  11904.0 um^2 total pattern area (2304.0 marks + 9600.0 interconnect).
- Layer-2 bounding box: (725, 885) - (1375, 1195) um.

## `dose_test.gds`

- Layer 1 only. Datatype 0 = one alignment mark (same cross style, reused
  at the bottom-right corner (1900.0, 100.0) — same coordinate as the BR
  mark above, ties this file's grid to the shared chip coordinate system).
  Datatype 1 = block pads, datatype 2 = block lines.
- A 5 x 5 grid of identical dose-test blocks, 40 um pitch, grid origin
  (lower-left of block (0,0)) at (1300.0, 200.0). Block (i, j), i, j = 0..4,
  lower-left corner at (1300 + 40j, 200 + 40i) um.
- Each block is 20 x 20 um and contains:
  - one 8 x 8 um pad, lower-left corner 1 um in from the block origin
    (area 64.0 um^2),
  - three lines of length 18 um and width 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 um, 1.5 um gaps
    (areas 9.0, 18.0, 36.0 um^2 — 63.0 um^2 total per block for the
    3 lines, 127.0 um^2 per block including the pad).
- Grid bounding box: (1300, 200) - (1480, 380) um (180 x 180 um).
- Cell `TOP`, 101 polygons total (100 block features + 1 mark), 3751.0 um^2
  total pattern area (3175.0 blocks + 576.0 mark).
